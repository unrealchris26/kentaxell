3. Why we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry. If you submit the booking form, we use your details to reply, discuss your event and, if you go ahead, to arrange the booking.
  • To send you marketing emails, only if you asked for them. See the next section.
  • To keep the site working and secure.

For legal review The lawful basis for each of the above depends on where your enquirers are located. Responding to an enquiry is usually justified under contract or legitimate interests; marketing generally requires consent. If you receive enquiries from the EU, the UK, or from California residents, the exact basis and the disclosures required differ. A qualified lawyer should confirm the wording for the jurisdictions you actually serve.

4. Marketing emails

We will only send you marketing emails if you explicitly opted in — either by subscribing through the newsletter form, or by ticking the separate, optional marketing box on the booking enquiry form.

Sending a booking enquiry does not subscribe you to marketing. If you submit an enquiry without ticking that box, we will only use your details to deal with your enquiry.

You can unsubscribe at any time using the unsubscribe link in the footer of any marketing email we send, or by emailing kentaxell@gmail.com and asking to be removed. Unsubscribing from marketing does not stop us replying to a booking enquiry you have made.

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the service providers needed to run the site and respond to you:

ProviderWhat it doesWhat it receives
Netlify Hosts the website and runs the code that processes form submissions Everything you submit, plus standard server log data including your IP address
HighLevel (LeadConnector) The customer relationship system where enquiries and subscriptions are stored Everything you submit through either form
Google Fonts Serves the typefaces used on this site Your IP address and browser information, because your browser requests the font files directly from Google

For legal review Google Fonts is loaded from Google's servers, so visitors' IP addresses are disclosed to Google on every page view. A German court has held that doing this without consent breached the GDPR. If you serve EU or UK visitors, the usual remedy is to self-host the font files so nothing is requested from Google. We can make that change on request — it is a small, self-contained job.

Needs your input Confirm whether you have a data processing agreement in place with HighLevel and Netlify. Both offer one. This matters if you handle personal information belonging to people in the EU or UK.

6. Cookies and tracking

This website does not set any cookies. It does not use local storage or session storage, and it contains no analytics, advertising or tracking scripts of any kind.

Because there are no non-essential cookies, there is no cookie consent banner. If tracking is added later — analytics, a Meta or TikTok pixel, a chat widget, an embedded video player — this section must be updated and a consent mechanism will need to be added before that tracking is allowed to load.

7. How long we keep it

Needs your input Tell us how long you keep enquiry records and newsletter subscribers, and we will state it here. A common approach is to keep booking records for as long as needed for tax and accounting purposes, and newsletter subscribers until they unsubscribe. We have deliberately not invented a period.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask us for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, to object to how we use it, or to withdraw consent you previously gave.

To make any of these requests, email kentaxell@gmail.com.

For legal review The specific rights you must offer, the deadlines for responding, and the wording required depend on jurisdiction — the GDPR in the EU, the UK GDPR, and state laws such as the CCPA/CPRA in California all differ, and Nevada has its own privacy statute. This section is written in general terms on purpose. A lawyer should confirm which regimes apply to you and tailor it.

9. International transfers

Our service providers are based in the United States, so if you contact us from outside the US your information will be transferred to and stored there.

For legal review If you receive enquiries from the EU or UK, transfers to the US need a valid transfer mechanism. Confirm this with a lawyer before relying on this section.

10. Security

The site is served over HTTPS, and form submissions are sent to our own server before being passed to our customer relationship system — the credentials for that system are never exposed to your browser. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Children

This site is intended for people booking entertainment services and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has submitted information to us, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change how this site collects or uses personal information, we will update this page and change the "last updated" date at the top.
