How a booking is secured

A booking is confirmed once a deposit of 50% of the agreed fee has been paid. The remaining balance is due no later than the day of the performance, unless agreed otherwise in writing. No payment is taken through this website — payment details are arranged directly.

If you cancel

Accepting a booking means the date is held exclusively for you and other work for that date is turned down. The deposit is therefore non-refundable. If you cancel, the following applies based on how much notice is given before the performance date:

Notice givenAmount payable
More than 60 daysThe deposit only — no further charge
30 to 60 days50% of the total agreed fee
Less than 30 days100% of the total agreed fee

Any travel or accommodation already booked and not recoverable is payable in addition, and will be evidenced on request.

Postponement

A confirmed booking may be moved once to an alternative date within 12 months, subject to availability, at no extra charge. Your deposit transfers to the new date. If no mutually workable date can be found, the booking is treated as a cancellation and the table above applies from the date you asked to postpone.

If we cancel for any reason other than one outside our control, you receive a full refund of everything paid, including the deposit. We will also make reasonable efforts to recommend a replacement performer of comparable standing, though we cannot guarantee one will be available.

Circumstances outside either party's control

Neither party is liable for failing to perform because of something genuinely outside their control — including serious illness or injury, bereavement, venue closure, government restrictions, extreme weather, or travel disruption.

Where this happens, both parties will first make reasonable efforts to reschedule. If rescheduling is not possible, you receive a refund of everything paid less any costs already incurred that cannot be recovered, which will be evidenced.

Refunds
